## 推荐新增示例

### Desk lamp

评论方向：

- light is too harsh
- cheap lamps flicker
- desk space is small
- hard to adjust angle
- late-night work needs softer lighting

### Pet hair vacuum

评论方向：

- pet hair sticks to couch
- normal vacuum misses corners
- loud vacuum scares pets
- filter gets clogged
- cleaning brush is annoying

### Balsamic vinegar

评论方向：

- salad tastes boring
- cheap vinegar tastes sour
- hard to find thick glaze
- bottle gets sticky
- want restaurant-style flavor at home

### Resistance bands

评论方向：

- gym is expensive
- bands snap easily
- hard to know which resistance to use
- workouts get boring
- need travel-friendly equipment

Product Description Mode

Best if you can describe the product, audience, and pain points yourself.

Use your own product copy and customer pain points to generate a Product-like creative brief. This mode uses `user_provided_description` as the source and does not call Amazon, Source Probe or Debug Mode.

Pasted Reviews Mode

Best if you have customer reviews, complaints, objections, or buyer feedback.

Paste customer reviews, complaints, objections, or buyer feedback. The creative brief will use these review snippets as user-provided evidence.

Input source: pasted customer feedback
If you do not have real reviews yet, use the sample reviews first. Later, paste buyer comments, objections, or complaints.
Paste customer reviews, complaints, objections, or buyer feedback.
Review count: 0. Paste at least 3 specific review snippets for stronger output.
Pain point preview: specific complaints will appear here.
What to paste

Paste real customer language: complaints, objections, review snippets, or things buyers wish were easier.

Good example
  • I hate cleaning my big blender every morning.
  • It is too loud for my apartment.
  • I only need one smoothie, not a huge blender jar.
Weak example
  • good product
  • nice
  • works well
Good review inputs include
  • customer complaints
  • review snippets
  • buyer objections
  • use-case frustrations
  • reasons people hesitate to buy
